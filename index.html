Sacred Cultural Legacy

Tradition & Heritage of Ningol Chakouba

Celebrated across Manipur and by the Manipuri diaspora worldwide, Ningol Chakouba (literally “Invitation to the Daughters for a Feast”) is the grandest socio-cultural festival dedicated to strengthening the sacred bond of love between married daughters (Ningols), their brothers, and their parental homes (Mapam).

Ancient Royal Roots

Tracing its lineage back to ancient Meitei history, and revived into a grand annual state tradition during the reign of Maharaja Chandrakirti in the 19th century, the festival began when queens and princesses invited their brothers and paternal clans for a ceremonial reunion feast.

The Grand Feast & Gifts

Dressed in radiant traditional Phanek and delicate Innaphi, Ningols arrive bearing seasonal fruits and sweets, while brothers and parents lovingly prepare an elaborate traditional feast and present cherished gifts (Khudol) as tokens of lifelong affection and support.

Blessings & Community

Following the feast, Ningols offer heartfelt prayers and blessings for the health, prosperity, and longevity of their brothers and parental families, reaffirming that no matter how far life takes them, their roots and belonging remain forever intact.
