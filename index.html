Motifs of Manipur

Woven in Tradition & Grace

Every element of our invitation reflects the sacred artistry of Manipur—honoring the timeless bond between Ningols (daughters & sisters) and their parental families.

Khamen Chatpa

The iconic royal scroll-and-medallion motif of Manipur, historically bestowed as a mark of honor, dignity, and cultural heritage.

Phanek & Thambal

Intricate handwoven Phanek borders paired with sacred Thambal (lotus) blossoms symbolizing purity, grace, and the warmth of sisterhood.

Pakhangba & Sanctum

The ancestral guardian emblem Pakhangba and golden-domed temple architecture invoke blessings of prosperity, harmony, and family unity.
